## 2. මුල් කවරය (Front Cover)

**Prompt:**
> Generate a high-resolution, sophisticated book cover design in a minimalistic style that merges System Engineering principles with Buddhist psychology. Interwoven within a glowing, intricate neural network of glowing circuitry is a silhouette of a serene, meditating figure in a seated posture, appearing to stabilize the entire system. The figure sits on a structure of lotus petals made of digital nodes. A path or architecture emerges from the figure, leading toward a radiating light source.
>
> **Text Integration:**
> - Main Title (Top): "පද්ධතිමය චිත්තවේගීය බුද්ධිය" (Systemic Emotional Intelligence) in clean, modern white Sinhalese font.
> - Subtitle (Below Title): "REFRAMED INSIGHTS" in uppercase Latin font.
> - Author (Bottom): "චින්තක දේශප්‍රිය ගුණදාස කතෘ / By Chinthaka Deshapriya Gunadasa" in professional font.

## 3. පසු කවරය (Back Cover)

**Prompt:**
> Generate a minimalistic back book cover design that seamlessly complements the front cover design. The color palette and visual language are identical. The central image is a conceptual landscape where floating, interlocking geometric modules (data points) are stabilized by an idealized tree with strong roots and lotus-patterned leaves, rooted in a tranquil pool.
>
> **Text Integration:**
> - Back-cover descriptive text blocks summarizing chapters (e.g., කම්පනය සහ චිත්තවේගී නැවත ඉගෙනීම, භාවවේගී නූගත්කමේ පිරිවැය, etc.).
> - Footer Text: "නොමිලේ ලබා දීම, ධර්ම දානය සඳහා පමණි."
> - Barcode Placeholder: A conceptual barcode placement at the bottom right.
